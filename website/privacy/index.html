Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026 · applies to the Laz Lights app for iPhone

Laz Lights does not collect any personal information. Your game progress stays on your device, purchases are handled by Apple, and nothing about you is sent to us or to anyone else.

Data at a glance

Type of dataWhat happens to it
Name, email, phone, or contactsNot collected
LocationNot collected
Photos, camera, or microphoneNot collected
Device or advertising identifiersNot collected
Usage data, analytics, or crash reportsNot collected
Payment detailsHandled by Apple
Game progress and settingsStays on your device

What stays on your device

To remember where you are in the game, Laz Lights saves the following in the app's own storage on your iPhone:

  • Levels reached, stars earned, and your ranked-run personal bests
  • Coins, tokens, lives, boosters, and power-ups
  • Daily streaks and Daily Signal progress
  • Your sound and music settings

This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

In-app purchases

Coin packs are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we never receive your card number, Apple Account details, or billing address. When a purchase completes, Apple tells the app which coin pack was bought so the coins can be added to your game.

Apple's handling of your payment information is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

No accounts, ads, or tracking

Laz Lights has no sign-in or user accounts. It shows no advertising, includes no analytics or tracking tools, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not sell or share any information.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. Like any website, the hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Children

Laz Lights is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Laz Lights changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
