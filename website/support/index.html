Support

Help with the Laz Lights app for iPhone

Found a bug, have a question, or have an idea for the game? Email us and we'll get back to you, usually within two business days.

lazlightssupport@gmail.com

It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

How do I play?

Drag your finger across 2 or more matching planets to link them, then lift your finger to collect them. Chains can bend and go diagonal. Each mission has goals shown at the top of the screen and a limited number of moves.

Link 6 or more planets to trigger a supernova, which clears the planets around it and hits bosses hard. Your first mission is a free Flight School tutorial that walks you through it.

How do lives work?

You can hold up to 5 lives. You lose one when you run out of moves in a mission, and one life refills every 20 minutes. The Daily Signal and the tutorial never cost a life.

I bought coins but they didn't show up

Make sure you're connected to the internet, then fully close Laz Lights and open it again. The App Store will deliver any purchase that didn't finish.

If the coins still aren't there, email us with the date of the purchase and the order ID from your Apple receipt email, and we'll sort it out.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple directly at reportaproblem.apple.com. Sign in with your Apple Account, find the purchase, and choose "Request a refund".

Will my progress move to a new iPhone?

Laz Lights has no accounts, so your levels, stars and coins are saved only on your iPhone. We can't see or recover them. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Coin packs are used up as you spend them, so they can't be restored on another device.

Which devices does Laz Lights work on?

Laz Lights is made for iPhone and needs iOS 17 or later. It plays offline, so you only need a connection to buy coin packs.

Does Laz Lights collect my data?

No. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. See the privacy policy for details.
