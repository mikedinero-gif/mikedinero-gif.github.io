Laz Lights

Link planets. Go supernova.

A neon space puzzle game for iPhone. Drag across matching planets to link them, chain six or more to set off a supernova, and battle your way across the galaxy to relight the long-dark Laz Lighthouse.

How it plays

Easy to pick up, hard to put down

🔗

Link and collect

Drag your finger across matching planets. Bend the chain, go diagonal, then lift to collect them all in a burst of light.

💥

Go supernova

Link six or more planets to trigger a supernova that blasts everything around it. It's your best weapon against bosses.

🔭

A story across the stars

Fly with Bloop, Nova and ZIP-7 through four sectors. Every star you earn rebuilds something: an observatory, a rescue pod, a listening dish, and finally the lighthouse.

📡

A new puzzle every day

The Daily Signal gives every player the same board each day, and it never costs a life. Ranked runs let you chase your weekly and monthly best.

Boss fights

Every sector ends in a showdown

  • Gloop the Moon EaterGobbles your planets and spits asteroids onto the board.
  • Captain VexTargets a row of your board and scrambles it.
  • The Void KrakenGrabs planets with its tentacles so you can't link them.
  • Sol TyrantCharges solar flares that rain down asteroids.

The board always shows what the boss is about to do. Counter it in time and the attack backfires.

No ads. No tracking. No account.
