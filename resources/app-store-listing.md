## Description (4000 max)
Link planets, light up the galaxy.

Laz Lights is a neon space puzzle game. Drag your finger across matching planets to link them into a chain. Bend it, go diagonal, keep it going. Lift your finger and the whole chain is collected in a burst of light. Link six or more and you trigger a SUPERNOVA that blasts everything around it.

A STORY ACROSS THE STARS
Fly with your crew, Bloop, Nova and ZIP-7, on a mission through four hand-built sectors. Rebuild Bloop's observatory in the Cradle Nebula, build a rescue pod for Pip in the Asteroid Reach, decode a mysterious signal in the Kraken Deep, and relight the ancient Laz Lighthouse in the Solar Forge. Every star you earn adds a piece to each sector's project.

BOSSES THAT FIGHT BACK
Each sector ends in a showdown:
• Gloop the Moon Eater gobbles your planets and spits asteroids
• Captain Vex targets a row of your board and scrambles it
• The Void Kraken grabs planets with its tentacles
• Sol Tyrant charges solar flares that rain down asteroids
Watch the board to see what the boss is about to do, counter it, and turn its own attack against it.

PUZZLES THAT KEEP EVOLVING
New planet types, asteroids and goals arrive as you go, from two kinds of planets to six. Plan your chain, build up a big cluster, then unleash it. Boosters like the Comet and Warp help when you're stuck, and they don't use a move.

PLAY EVERY DAY
• Daily Signal: one new puzzle each day, the same board for every player, and it never costs a life
• Ranked runs: chase your personal best each week and month
• Galaxy journal: collect everything you discover along the way
• Uncharted space: beyond the lighthouse, new sectors keep coming

MADE TO RELAX OR CHALLENGE
• Easy to learn with a short Flight School tutorial
• Glowing neon visuals and satisfying chain effects
• Plays offline. No account, no sign-in
• No ads and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device

Laz Lights is free to play. Optional coin packs are available as in-app purchases.

Grab your ship, cadet. The lighthouse is waiting.
